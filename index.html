description Project Overview

Talkonlearn is a mobile app that helps students improve their speaking, pronunciation, and public speaking confidence, using AI to evaluate their speech in real-time. Built specifically to cater to student communication challenges, it bridges the gap between fear of speaking in public and personal mastery through non-judgmental, immediate feedback.

error_outline The Problem

Many students feel nervous speaking in front of others and get little feedback when they practice alone. As IT students frequently tasked with presentations, we saw firsthand how lack of accessible, objective feedback discourages learners from practicing consistently.

lightbulb The Solution

Short speaking drills where the app gives feedback on your speech. It tracks your progress over time and keeps you motivated with streaks and achievement badges, turning tedious speech rehearsal into an engaging daily routine.

BookYourCalm — Case Study

Wellness & Spa Appointment Booking Web Portal

person Sole Full-Stack Developer & Designer • End-to-End Responsibility
Application Visuals

description Project Overview

A responsive web portal designed for streamlined wellness and spa appointment bookings, empowering clients with full visibility over therapist availability and appointment schedules. Built to provide a serene, friction-free booking journey from initial discovery to session confirmation.

error_outline The Problem

Booking a massage or wellness session often requires phone calls or back-and-forth messaging, with no clear real-time view of available therapists, scheduling slots, or past session records. I chose this problem to eliminate scheduling friction and make booking self-service, calm, and transparent for clients and practitioners alike.

lightbulb The Solution

An intuitive step-by-step booking flow where clients select a specialized service, choose a licensed therapist of choice, pick convenient dates and times, and confirm instantly. Clients can then effortlessly track upcoming and past sessions directly within their personalized client wellness dashboard.
